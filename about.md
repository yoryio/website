I'm **yoryo** — a cyber threat intelligence analyst and researcher focused on how
intrusions actually begin: attack surface, adversary infrastructure, initial access,
and the OPSEC that ties them together.

My work sits in the middle ground between threat intelligence and offensive security:
tracking adversary infrastructure, reproducing and analyzing N-day vulnerabilities to
understand initial-access tradecraft, and turning that understanding into detection and
defense. I have a particular interest in threats targeting **Latin America** — an
under-reported region — and in the commercial-spyware ecosystem.

This blog is where I publish infrastructure-tracking write-ups, technical analysis, and
notes from lab research. Everything here is for defensive research, detection, and
authorized testing.

You can find my tools and code on [GitHub](https://github.com/yoryio).
